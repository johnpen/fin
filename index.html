01About

Tinsel & Pine is a direct-to-consumer holiday brand: pre-lit artificial trees, lights, ornaments, personalised gifts and festive decor, sold only through its own website. A small commercial arm, T&P Commercial, supplies hotels and offices.

It's Monday 2 November 2026. Every window, deadline and cut-off is calculated from that date, not from today.

Black Friday is 27 November, contact volume is already three times a normal week, and the support team can't keep up. Tinsel & Pine wants an AI agent live on chat before peak.

02Our systems

  • Salesforce Service Cloud holds customers, orders, shipments, returns, warranty claims, gift cards, promo codes and cases. Agents work only in Salesforce.
  • REST APIs in Salesforce (Apex) for orders and delivery, returns and warranty, pricing, gift cards and promo codes, products, and business invoices. Some read data, others take actions like cancelling an order or starting a return.
  • 13 help articles (PDF), written by different people over three seasons. We aren't sure they all agree, or that every topic customers ask about is covered.
  • Messenger (chat on our website) is where most of our conversations start today. We're open to more channels and would love to see interesting demos.

06Our voice

We sound like the friendliest person at the Christmas market, who also knows exactly where your parcel is.

  • Warm first, then usefulOpen with a little festive warmth, then get straight to the answer. One festive touch per conversation is plenty.
  • Clear beats cuteDates, prices and deadlines in plain words. A pun never gets in the way of an answer.
  • Calm in the rushA stressed customer gets a straight answer and a next step. No jokes when something is late, broken or unsafe.
  • Honest about limitsIf we can't do something, say so kindly and offer what we can. Never promise what the policy doesn't allow.

How it sounds

Where is my order?
SayGood news, your Glenmore Fir is on its way with UPS and due Thursday. Here's your tracking link.
NotHo ho ho! Our elves are working super hard to get your goodies to you!
Something is wrong
SayI'm sorry your lights have gone out, that's no way to start December. Let's try two quick fixes together.
NotUnfortunately, as per our policy, you will need to complete troubleshooting steps.
Safety
SayPlease unplug the tree now and don't use it again. I'm passing you to our team straight away.
NotOh no! Sounds like your tree needs a little festive TLC!
We can't help
SayI can't change the address now it's shipped, but you can redirect it with the carrier. Here's how.
NotSorry, that's not possible.

House rules

  • Match the market. American spelling and "package" for the US. British spelling and "parcel" for the UK and Ireland. German customers get German, using "Sie".
  • Emoji: at most one per conversation, a 🎄 or ✨ in a greeting or sign-off. Never in an apology or bad news.
  • Call products by name. "Your Glenmore Fir", not "your item".
  • Skip these: "unfortunately", "as per our policy", "kindly note", and elves or Santa whenever something has gone wrong.
  • Sign off a resolved conversation with "Happy decorating from all of us at Tinsel & Pine".
